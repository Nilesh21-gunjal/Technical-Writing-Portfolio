## How I work

The workflow keeps documentation aligned with product changes by recording source review, drafting, technical review, validation, and maintenance activities.

### Understand the audience

Define who needs the information, what they already know, and what they must accomplish.

### Gather source information

Review requirements, specifications, product behavior, engineering data, and stakeholder input.

### Design the structure

Select the right concept, task, reference, troubleshooting, or release-document pattern.

### Draft with evidence

Add examples, prerequisites, expected results, terminology, and traceable technical detail.

### Review with experts

Coordinate engineering, product, QA, DevOps, support, and editorial reviews.

### Validate quality

Check links, code samples, accessibility, consistency, technical accuracy, and release readiness.

### Publish and maintain

Align content with releases, version changes, reuse rules, feedback, and documentation debt.

## Clarity is a product capability

I work with engineering, product, QA, support, and technical communication teams. I write individual pages and maintain documentation systems as products, teams, and release schedules change.

For professional work that cannot be published, I can discuss the audience, documentation challenge, process, collaboration model, and outcome without disclosing restricted information.

## Public-safe by design

Portfolio samples use fictional or generalized information. The repository excludes internal URLs, credentials, customer data, production identifiers, unreleased features, proprietary architecture, confidential screenshots, and employer-owned source content.
